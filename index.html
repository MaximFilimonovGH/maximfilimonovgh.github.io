Postdoctoral Research Associate

Dr Maxim Filimonov

School of Computational and Mathematical Sciences • Cardiff University

I am a Research Associate within Cardiff NLP Group exploring the potential of NLP for real-world applications, as well as enhancing the understanding of NLP technology and language models. I am dedicated to finding creative solutions and have a genuine curiosity and enthusiasm to work with a multi-disciplinary research team in computer science.

Previously, I completed my PhD in Model-Based Systems Engineering at Birmingham City University, and I have been working on a variety of projects as a Research Software Engineer at Cardiff University since 2020.

Current & Past Projects

PROJECT 01 • NLP & AI TEXT ANNOTATION Active Focus

Automatic Text Annotation using LLMs

Web application for automatic annotation of text documents using custom labels.

Keywords: NLP LLM Python Fullstack Development
PROJECT 02 • GREAT Cross-Disciplinary

Global Real-time Early Assessment of Tsunamis (GREAT)

Tsunami warning technology towards a global real-time analysis. The technology is based on the analysis of acoustic signals generated together with the tsunami, due to the compression of the water layer.

Keywords: Python Machine Learning Acoustic-Gravity Waves
